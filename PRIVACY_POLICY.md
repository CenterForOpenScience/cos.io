## CENTER FOR OPEN SCIENCE, INC.

## 1. INTRODUCTION
Center for Open Science, Inc. (referred to as "COS", "OSF", "us" "our" and "we") is a nonprofit culture change organization that aims to align scholarly practices with scholarly values. It does so by developing and advancing a systems strategy to increase rigor, transparency, and sharing of research. COS fosters an open and interactive community among open source developers, open science researchers, and the broader scientific community by providing tools, training, and services that support transparent study planning, output and outcome sharing, and collaboration across research communities.

Center for Open Science, Inc. is affiliated with Center for Open Science AISBL, an international nonprofit association organized under Belgian law ("COS AISBL"). Unless a specific Service states otherwise, the Websites and Services, including OSF, are provided by Center for Open Science, Inc.

The COS mission is to increase the openness, integrity, and trustworthiness of research. To meet that objective, COS employs a systems strategy involving technology to make open practices possible and easy, community building to make open practices normative, and stakeholder engagement to make open practices rewarding and required. COS built, maintains and operates the OSF, a free, open source infrastructure (referred to as "OSF"), for helping researchers register study plans, publish preprints through community-run services, and link related data, materials, code, and outcomes wherever they live, creating transparent, connected research records. This Privacy Policy applies to contributors of content to, and users of, our Websites and Services.

In this document, we use "you" to refer to researchers and other Active Users of our Websites and Services.
